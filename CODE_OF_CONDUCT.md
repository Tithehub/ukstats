# Code of Conduct

UKStats follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.

## The short version

- Be respectful and constructive. Disagree about data and code, not about people.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- This project deals with politically sensitive statistics (migration, crime, speech prosecutions). Contributions must stay factual and sourced — advocacy belongs elsewhere.
- Maintainers may remove contributions and block contributors who break these rules.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to **admin@xpre.ai**. All reports are reviewed and kept confidential.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant 2.1 applies:
