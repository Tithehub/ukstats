- Share cards (/api/public/og/$key) render in pure JS via og-render.server.ts from published snapshots — no native image libs on the edge.
- Globe quakes/satellites: earth-watch.functions.ts + pure quakes.ts/satellites.ts; natural-event and orbit layers only, never figures.
- Scandals: scandals.data.ts snapshot; official records separate from labelled news/YouTube; summaries state what an official body found.
- Globe public cameras: public-cams.ts parsers (registry CAM_SOURCES; TfL, Caltrans, DriveBC, Fintraffic, Iceland, Hong Kong, NZTA, Lithuania) + public-cams.functions.ts; public-authority open data only; only Caltrans HLS streams are labelled LIVE, TfL loops and stills say so; scene only, never figures.
- Globe weather: weather-globe.ts city list/parser + getWeather in earth-watch.functions.ts (Open-Meteo, 30 min); labelled model readings, never register figures.
- Education (src/lib/education*.ts, table education_institutions, payload_cache "education-v1", nightly /api/public/education-tick): DfE GIAS register, Ofsted MI, EES national/KS4 data and the OfS Register only; suppressed cells stay "Not published", Ofsted report-card and older graded outcomes are never merged, devolved nations show "No live feed" links — keeps every school figure traceable to its publisher.
- Places (src/lib/places*.ts, payload_cache "places-v1", nightly /api/public/places-tick): ONS well-being, UK HPI, ASHE, mid-year population, Ofsted (county figure for districts) and ONS crime per council; the /move-compare score is labelled unofficial, drops a factor missing for either place, and never feeds register figures, embeds, reports or the stream.
