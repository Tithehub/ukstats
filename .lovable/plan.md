# Add four more scandals

## New entries (working titles)
1. **"The disappearance of Madeleine McCann"** (International) — she went missing in Praia da Luz, Portugal, in May 2007. Covers the Met Police's Operation Grange, the German prosecutors' case against their suspect, and any update in 2026.
   - She is described as missing. Anyone named is described only as a suspect, charged, acquitted or cleared, exactly as the authorities say. Her parents are described as no longer suspects, as the Portuguese authorities confirmed in 2008.
2. **"The death of John McAfee"** (International) — the antivirus founder was found dead in a Barcelona prison in June 2021 while awaiting extradition to the US. Covers the US Justice Department charges, the Spanish court's extradition ruling, and the finding on his cause of death.
3. **"Julian Assange and WikiLeaks"** (UK and US) — the leaks, his time in the Ecuadorian embassy, his years in Belmarsh prison, the UK extradition rulings, and his 2024 plea deal in Saipan and release.
   - Official: US Justice Department, UK court judgments, the US court plea record.
4. **"Edward Snowden and the NSA surveillance leaks"** (UK and US) — the 2013 leaks, the US espionage charges, his asylum and later Russian citizenship, and the official reviews that followed (for example the US Privacy and Civil Liberties Oversight Board, and the European Court of Human Rights ruling on GCHQ in Big Brother Watch v UK).

## Rules kept
- Every link checked before adding. Summaries say only what an official body or court found. News headlines are verbatim, dated and labelled non-official.
- Photos are freely licensed pictures of places, never of the people (for example Praia da Luz, the Brians 2 prison area or Barcelona, the Ecuadorian embassy in London, the NSA headquarters), marked "Illustrative only".
- They appear in the Scandals list, the globe Scandals section and the sitemap automatically.

## Technical
- Add four records to `src/lib/scandals.data.ts`, upload the photos and add them to `scandals.images.ts`, then run `scandals.test.ts`.
