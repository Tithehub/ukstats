- Portrait: above the figure card.

1. Publish the site. This also puts live the 2026 footage matched to each figure, the Top 6 box on the globe and the UKStats Live box on the home page.
2. Load the published stream page at 1920x1080 and check that the Top 6 panel shows and nothing is cut off.
3. You, in OBS: double-click the "UKStats" Browser source and click "Refresh cache of current page". The panel should then show on YouTube within a few seconds.
