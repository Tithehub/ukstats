# Ways to make money once the payment pause lifts

All statistics stay free forever. Nothing paid ever changes, hides or ranks a figure. Every paid item is labelled "Sponsor, not official data".

## 1. Live stream sponsors (biggest earner)
- **Title sponsor, £299/month (one slot):** named by the presenter in every 15-minute "About UKStats" break, plus their own corner box with a QR code linking to their site.
- **Stream sponsor, £149/month (up to 5 slots):** shown in the rotating corner box with their own QR code, and named in the break.
- Target: 1 title sponsor + 3 stream sponsors = about £750 a month.

## 2. Topic page sponsors (£99–£199/month each)
- One sponsor per topic page: housing, cost of living, debt and gold, preparedness, small boats, spending.
- A small "Supported by" strip on that page only. Banned categories still apply.
- Target: 4 pages = about £500 a month.

## 3. Business supporter directory (£49/month)
- Logo and link on the public sponsors page and in the website footer rotation.
- Easy for local firms to buy without talking to anyone.

## 4. Individual supporters (£5/month or £50/year)
- A thank-you badge and their name on the supporters wall, if they choose. This brings in goodwill more than money.

## 5. Founding sponsor offer (first 90 days)
- The first 10 sponsors get 25% off for life and a "Founding sponsor" label. This gives people a reason to sign up early.

## 6. Media kit page
- A public /advertise pack with live YouTube viewer and site visitor numbers (real figures only, dated), example screenshots and a one-click Stripe checkout.

## 7. Outreach (done by you, with drafts from me)
- A list of 30 suitable UK businesses (yacht brokers, gold dealers, pharmacies, preparedness suppliers, mortgage brokers) with a ready-to-send email and X message. Sending stays manual.

## Left out on purpose
- Paid data downloads or a "Pro" plan: this clashes with keeping the statistics free forever. I'll only add it if you ask.
- Google display ads: low earnings, and they can show categories we've banned.

## What happens when the pause lifts
1. Turn payments back on with one switch, and remove the legal-hold notice.
2. Set up the new prices in Stripe (title, stream, topic, business and individual).
3. Paid sponsors join the stream rotation and pages automatically after admin approval.
4. Add links to the media kit from the menu, the home page, the sponsors page and the stream QR.

## Technical notes
- The single pause flag in the funding policy file turns checkout and the notice back on together.
- New Stripe prices are created in test mode and copied to live when the site is published.
- Topic sponsors use the existing sponsor records, with a topic field and admin approval.
