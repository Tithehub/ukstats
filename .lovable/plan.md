Orbit draft: globe glow, sponsor card and subtle 3D panels (preview only, nothing published)

Matching the right-hand reference image. The old live stream, ukstats.co.uk and the YouTube/OBS setup stay unchanged.

1. Globe lighting
- Brighter, warmer gold city lights with soft bloom clusters over Europe/UK, like the reference.
- Glowing blue network arcs with moving light pulses between cities.
- Stronger warm sun glare at the top right and a crisp, brighter blue edge glow around the globe.
- Slightly deeper ocean blues so the lights stand out.

2. Sponsor card, laid out like the reference
- Top half: bold uppercase title on the left over a full-bleed picture that fills the right side and fades softly into the panel (photo sponsors fill it; book covers are shown whole on a blurred copy of themselves, so they never look like a thin strip).
- Thin divider line, then QR code bottom-left with a two-line bold uppercase call to action and the web address beside it.
- Keeps its current angle and tuck behind the presenter.

3. Subtle 3D on all panels
- A faint top-edge highlight, a thin bevel along the sides, a soft inner glow and a gentle drop shadow beneath each panel, plus a very slow light sweep, so panels look like glass slabs rather than flat boxes.
- Next Up and the right figure card stay square-on; the effect is depth, not tilt.

- Then share the draft link for you to review before any OBS/YouTube step.
