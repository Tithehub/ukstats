# Home page: three new modern examples

Pictures only, nothing on the site changes. The aim is a modern, clean look that still hits hard: big live counters, bold news-channel headlines, icons on everything, pictures and maps, and plenty of space. Each example comes as a desktop and a phone picture, using today's real figures.

**D. Live national dashboard**
- A huge debt counter in the headline, with a fine grid background and a soft glow.
- An icon row for the six key figures: debt, small boats, terror level, weather, inflation and crime. Each has a large number and a coloured status dot.
- A small map of the Channel next to the boats figure, and a UK map with the weather warning areas next to the weather card.

**E. Bold news channel**
- A slim red "LIVE" bar with a ticker of official headlines.
- One large lead card with an icon, the figure and an image. Today that's the small boats card, with a Channel picture.
- Below it, a bento grid of image tiles: terror level, weather, debt, inflation, crime and spending.

**F. Clean and minimal**
- Lots of white space and a light mode look in Restore Britain navy and white.
- Three giant numbers (debt, boats, terror level) with line icons and simple sparklines.
- Topic icons in a neat grid, and paid services in one quiet card at the bottom.

All three keep:
- Every current feature (globe, stream, countries, spending, the inquiry page, preparedness, sponsors, Rent), placed lower down.
- "Free forever" near the top, with paid services second.
- The source and date on every figure, and "Not published" where a figure doesn't exist.

## Technical details
- HTML mockups with lucide-style inline icons, Archivo and IBM Plex Mono, real values from the register page, and pictures generated as decoration only (no figures in images). Captured at 1440 and 375 widths to /mnt/documents/home-layouts/v2/.
