# Live stream design concepts and new YouTube thumbnail (preview only)

The site stays exactly as it is. These are picture mockups saved to your Files for you to review. Nothing gets built until you pick one.

## What you get

1. **Three stream layout concepts** (1920x1080 stills, all in the navy Restore Britain palette with red and yellow highlights):
   - **A. "Newsroom pulse"**: a slowly moving map of the UK behind the presenter. The area being discussed lights up and soft data lines flow towards it. The headline ticker sits along the bottom, and the Top 6 panel and the bottom-left sponsor card stay where they are.
   - **B. "Data wall"**: a deep-navy studio wall made of glowing number tiles. When she reads a stat, its tile grows, and a large animated chart (bars or a line) draws itself behind her.
   - **C. "Globe orbit"**: the live globe turns slowly behind her and zooms to the country in each headline. Light rings pulse on the place being mentioned.
   For each concept, a short note explains how the backdrop changes for each type of story: stat, headline, weather warning, small boats, sponsor break.

2. **One short motion sample** (around 8 seconds) of the concept that looks most animated, so you can see the movement rather than imagine it.

3. **Three YouTube live thumbnails** (1280x720), each built around the real UKStats logo, a red LIVE badge, and the words "UK STATS LIVE · 24/7". Styles:
   - a bold headline style,
   - a globe with the UK highlighted,
   - a presenter silhouette over a data wall.

## Rules kept
- Any numbers in the mockups are shown as obvious placeholders (for example "00,000"), never made-up stats.
- No news-outlet footage or logos.
- Files are saved to Files only. No site changes.
