The AI video tool has refused to animate stills that show a realistic presenter, so I'll build this as a code-animated broadcast instead. Your exact image is the base, and every graphic on it moves:

- The globe slowly turns, with a soft glow and twinkling city lights.

- The "World headline" card flips through placeholder headlines, and its "00,000" number counts up each time.
- The "Next up" list moves from UK debt to small boats to weather, highlighting each in turn.

- The "UK STATS LIVE · WORLD" bar slides in, and the sponsor card swaps to a second card partway through.
- The presenter stays still. Your real animated presenter would sit over this on the actual stream.
