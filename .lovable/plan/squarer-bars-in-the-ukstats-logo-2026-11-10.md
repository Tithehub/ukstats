# Squarer bars in the UKStats logo

The three bars are currently fully rounded at the ends (corner radius is half the bar width). Change them to very slightly rounded corners.

## What changes
- Bar corner radius goes from 10 to 2 (about 10% of the bar width): mostly square, with just a soft edge.
- Bar sizes, spacing, lettering and colours stay exactly the same.
- Applies to all versions: navy on white, white on navy, both transparent versions and both square icons.

## Delivery
- Saved as new files with `_v2` in the name inside the `ukstats_logo` folder (originals kept): SVG plus 4000 px PNG for each logo, 1024 px PNG for each icon.
- Each PNG is checked at full size and zoomed on the bar corners before delivery.
- The website itself is not changed.
