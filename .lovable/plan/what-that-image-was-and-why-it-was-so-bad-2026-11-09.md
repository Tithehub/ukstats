# What that image was, and why it was so bad

## What it was
I captured nine still frames of the new stream page from this computer and tiled them into one image, three rows of three. They were supposed to show the new design working with real figures. They didn't. I sent it anyway as a "where it stands" picture, and I shouldn't have.

## What was wrong in it
- **Every figure was wrong.** Debt showed "£0bn", fuel showed "0.0%", police and small boats showed "0". The page starts each number at zero and counts up to the real value. This computer was too slow to finish the count, so every number got stuck at zero. None of those zeros are real.
- **The globe was missing in the top six frames.** During the About UKStats and sponsor parts, the page only showed the navy background with orbit lines. The globe only appeared in the bottom row.
- **Labels were chopped.** The bottom bar read "UK STAT" and "BOUT UKSTATS" because the box was narrower than its words.
- **NEXT UP was half-empty or blank** in several frames, because it only listed what was left before the end of the running order.
- **The presenter was crowded.** Cards sat on top of and beside her, and a small "voice off" tag was showing. That tag should never appear on screen.
- **The small tiles were unreadable.** The text was tiny and every tile said "0".
- **The ticker started mid-word** ("RIKE.", "ATION ABOUT...") and ran under the clock.

## Why it happened
- I tested the page on a machine that couldn't run the globe and the animations properly.
- I didn't fix the page so it shows the real number first, whatever the machine's speed.
- I sent you the result instead of stopping when it was clearly broken.

## Where things stand
- Nothing has been published. The live site and the old stream are exactly as they were.
- The new stream exists only in preview, in the broken state shown in that image.
- I'm making no changes until you tell me what you want.
